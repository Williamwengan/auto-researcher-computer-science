# Anonymous Submission Checklist

- [ ] No author names, affiliations, acknowledgements, grant numbers, or personal email addresses.
- [ ] No local absolute paths or usernames in text, figures, metadata, or logs.
- [ ] Anonymous repository and neutral self-citation wording.
- [ ] PDF metadata inspected.
- [ ] Dataset/API licenses and model versions documented.
- [ ] Generative-AI assistance documented as required without revealing identity.
- [ ] Seven content pages maximum; total maximum nine pages with pages after seven used only for references, per AAAI-27 CFP.
- [ ] Reproducibility checklist included.
- [ ] Official AAAI-27 Author Kit used.
