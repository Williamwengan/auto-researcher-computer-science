# Si et al. 2025 Style Research Idea Review Instruction

Please review each research idea using a 1-10 score.

## Review Dimensions

### 1. Novelty
Is the idea creative and meaningfully different from existing baselines?

- 1-3: mostly repeats existing work
- 4-6: somewhat new, but mainly incremental
- 7-8: clearly different from existing baselines
- 9-10: highly original and potentially opens a new direction

### 2. Feasibility
Can the idea be implemented and evaluated with reasonable time, data, and compute?

- 1-3: unrealistic or missing key data/resources
- 4-6: possible but with unclear implementation details
- 7-8: feasible with clear data, baselines, and metrics
- 9-10: very feasible and well-scoped

### 3. Expected Effectiveness
How likely is the method to improve over baselines or produce useful findings?

- 1-3: unlikely to improve or answer the research question
- 4-6: may improve some metrics, but uncertain
- 7-8: likely to improve over clear baselines
- 9-10: very likely to produce strong measurable gains

### 4. Excitement
If successful, would this idea be interesting or impactful?

- 1-3: minor or narrow contribution
- 4-6: useful but not very exciting
- 7-8: interesting and potentially publishable
- 9-10: very exciting, strong research potential

### 5. Overall
Overall quality of the research idea.

- 1-3: weak idea
- 4-5: has some value but clear issues
- 6: decent and possibly worth trying
- 7-8: strong idea worth prioritizing
- 9-10: excellent idea
